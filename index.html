The gap

Right now, getting sick in Bali is improvisation.

A hotel concierge who knows someone. A pharmacy that may or may not stock what you need. A clinic that quotes tourists a different price.

At 2am, in a villa, in a country whose medical system you have never used and whose language you do not speak, improvisation is not a plan. It is the reason people fly home early, or worse, wait.

BaliDoc replaced the improvisation with one system: a licensed doctor, a real prescription, a licensed pharmacy, and a delivery, all on one case file.
